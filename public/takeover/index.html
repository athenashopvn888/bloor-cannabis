Frequently asked questions

What are your hours?

We're open Monday to Thursday and Sunday from 11 AM to 11 PM, and Friday to Saturday from 11 AM to 3 AM. Walk in anytime — no appointment needed.

What product categories do you carry?

Five tiers of flower from exotic to budget, plus pre-rolls, edibles, vapes, concentrates and accessories. The menu on this site is synced to our live inventory.

Do I need to be 19 to enter?

Yes — 19+ only, with valid government ID checked in store, every visit, no exceptions.

Can I reserve online?

Yes. Hit Reserve on any available product, leave your name and number, and we'll hold it for pickup — you pay in store after ID check.

Where exactly are you, and is there parking?

993 Bloor St W in Toronto (M6H 1M1), on the Bloorcourt strip. Free evening street parking is usually easy to find nearby.
